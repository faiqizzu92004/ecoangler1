A little about this forecast

EcoAngler combines live weather forecasts with Random Forest and XGBoost. The two models' category probabilities are averaged equally to estimate Poor, Good, or High productivity. These categories describe catches per visitor in the training data, rather than a predicted number of fish.

The model was trained on fishing reports from Honmoku, Japan. Estimates for Malaysia are experimental: different species, fishing grounds, and seasons can behave differently. Forecasts are a planning aid and have not been validated as local fishing advice.

This website model uses weather and calendar inputs, so it does not need daily catch reports. Water temperature, waves, and sea level provide extra context and do not feed this model.

Observed weather was used for training; predictions made with weather forecasts need further prospective evaluation. Check local conditions, access rules, and weather advisories before heading out.

Weather and ocean data: Open-Meteo, using free public APIs, with marine forecasts from DWD (CC BY 4.0). Sea level includes tides and is relative to mean sea level; it is not a local tide gauge or a navigation aid. Marine data availability varies by location.
